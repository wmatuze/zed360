# Zed360 brand identity

## Brand idea

Zed360 connects a customer's need with a complete view of relevant businesses.
The slightly rotated rounded square suggests movement and approachability. The
bold `Z` provides instant recognition at small sizes, while the small highlight
suggests a useful discovery rather than decoration.

## Core assets

- `/brand/zed360-mark.svg` is the standalone icon for avatars, app shortcuts,
  favicons, and compact placements.
- `/brand/zed360-wordmark-dark.svg` combines the mark and wordmark for use on
  dark backgrounds.
- The `BrandLogo` React component is the source used by the application UI.
- `app/icon.svg`, `app/manifest.ts`, and `app/opengraph-image.tsx` provide the
  browser, installable-app, and social-sharing identities.

## Colours

- **Zed Lime** `#B8F238`: primary action and recognition colour.
- **Zed Ink** `#0B0D12`: main background and the letter inside the mark.
- **Signal Sky** `#77D8FF`: secondary informational accent.
- **White** `#FFFFFF`: primary text on dark surfaces.

Zed Lime should remain an accent rather than a large page background. This
protects contrast and makes important actions easy to identify.

## Wordmark

Write the name as `Zed360`, with no space. In the application wordmark, `Zed`
is white and `360` is Zed Lime. Do not change the casing, stretch the mark, add
outlines, or place it on a visually busy image without a dark protective area.

## Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space around the full logo equal to at least one quarter of the
mark's width. Use the standalone mark below 120 CSS pixels of available
horizontal space. Do not render the mark smaller than 24 by 24 CSS pixels.

## Meaning and trust

The brand must not imply that Zed360 guarantees a business, transaction, or
review. Approval and verification wording must continue to state exactly what
was checked.
