# Public business comparison

Zed360 lets a visitor compare two or three approved public businesses without creating an account.

## Product rules

- A visitor can select up to three businesses from the public directory.
- Selections are saved only in that browser using local storage. They are not attached to a customer account or sent to Zed360 as a saved list.
- The comparison link contains public business slugs, so it can be shared and opened on another device without signing in.
- The page compares public information that is current when the page loads. It does not preserve a historical snapshot.
- Zed360 presents comparable facts and trust signals but does not calculate an overall score, rank the businesses, or recommend a winner.

## Information shown

- Availability and fulfilment options
- Zed360 approval and verification labels
- Public rating and review count
- Locations and operating hours
- Services and their fixed, starting, or range prices
- Public products and their prices
- Public contact options

Business-provided information must remain distinguishable from checks performed by Zed360 or an external authority. The comparison must never imply that business approval verifies every claim, price, product, or review.

## Privacy and safety

Only information already approved for a public business profile may appear in a comparison. Private requests, customer contact details, internal review notes, unpublished products, and pending or rejected media must never be included.
