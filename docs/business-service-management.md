# Business service management

Owners and managers maintain services at `/business/[businessId]/services`.
They can add services, edit descriptions and price guidance, update current
availability, and archive services that are no longer offered. Staff accounts
remain read-only.

Each service belongs to an active Zed360 category. Service names must be unique
within a business, and the pilot permits up to 50 active services per business.
Archiving is reversible and preserves coverage configuration. Archived or
unavailable services are excluded from public profiles and request matching.

Coverage remains a separate workflow because where and how a service is
fulfilled is different from what the business offers. Newly created services
therefore link owners to the existing coverage editor for at-business,
pickup, travel, delivery, and remote fulfilment.

Price values are owner-provided guidance in Zambian kwacha. They are not
quotes, invoices, or payment obligations, and Zed360 does not process service
payments.

## Deferred scope

Direct video hosting and payment processing are intentionally outside the
near-term roadmap. The image-only catalog and direct business contact model
remain the product boundary during the pilot. Reconsidering either capability
requires a separate product, safety, infrastructure, and operating-cost review.
