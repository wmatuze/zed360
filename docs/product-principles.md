# Zed360 product principles

## Vision

Build Zambia's definitive digital infrastructure for discovering, evaluating, and connecting with legitimate businesses.

## What Zed360 is

Zed360 is a digital business ecosystem in which:

- customers express real needs;
- businesses describe their capabilities and current operating state;
- the platform connects relevant demand and supply;
- trust grows from verification and demonstrated behaviour;
- customers and businesses transact directly;
- every confirmed interaction improves future discovery and reputation.

## What Zed360 is not

- Not a static catalogue of company names and telephone numbers
- Not a pay-to-win ranking system
- Not an online shop or payment intermediary
- Not a paid service: Zed360 takes no payments of any kind
- Not a claim that registration alone guarantees service quality
- Not a collection of unverified public reviews

## Product test

Every feature should answer at least one of these questions:

1. Does it help a customer express or solve a need?
2. Does it help a relevant business respond?
3. Does it improve the freshness or reliability of information?
4. Does it create a legitimate trust signal?
5. Does it make future matching or decision-making better?

If a feature only makes the platform look like a larger list of businesses, it is not a priority.

## Core success measure

The principal measure is the number and percentage of customer needs that receive a useful business response and result in a confirmed connection. Listing count and page views are supporting measures, not the definition of success.

## No payments

Zed360 takes no payments from businesses or customers. There are no paid
featured listings, paid advertisements, subscriptions, boosts, or checkout.

- Visibility is earned through confirmed interactions, responsiveness,
  reputation, verification, and current information. Money must never change
  where a business appears.
- Any "featured", "spotlight", or "recently verified" section is selected by
  published merit rules or editorial judgement, is free, and is labelled with
  the reason a business appears.
- Customers pay businesses directly, outside Zed360. Product and service prices
  are displayed for information only.
