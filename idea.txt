# What is Zed360?

Zed360 is a **national business discovery and reputation platform**.

Notice I didn't call it a business directory.

Business directories already exist.

We are trying to build something much bigger.

Think of it as:

> **"The digital home of every legitimate business in Zambia."**

# Does it contribute anything?

This is the most important question you've asked.

If Zed360 disappeared before it existed...

Would Zambia lose anything?

Today?

No.

Because people have adapted.

They use:

* Facebook
* WhatsApp groups
* Google
* Friends
* Walking around
* Asking people

But that's not the right comparison.

The real question is:

> **Would Zambia be better off if Zed360 existed?**

I believe the answer is **yes**, but only if we execute it exceptionally well.

Why?

Because Zed360 isn't trying to replace Facebook or Google.

It's trying to become the **trusted business discovery layer for Zambia**.

Think about services people use every day:

* Google Maps helps people navigate.
* LinkedIn helps professionals connect.
* TripAdvisor helps travelers choose.
* Booking.com helps people find accommodation.
* Yelp helps people discover local businesses.

None of those created the businesses—they made it dramatically easier to find and trust them.

Zed360 can play that role for Zambia by focusing on local needs that global platforms don't fully address.

## One thing I think we should improve

Looking back at all our discussions, I think we can make the vision even stronger.

Instead of describing Zed360 as **"a business directory"**, we should describe it as:

> **Zed360 is Zambia's Business Discovery Platform.**

Or even better:

> **Zed360 is Zambia's Digital Business Ecosystem.**

Those descriptions better capture what we're building. A directory is just a list. An ecosystem connects businesses, customers, information, trust, reputation, and discovery.

---

### I also think we should redefine our long-term mission.

Originally, we wanted to build something better than BizBwana.

I think we've outgrown that goal.

The new mission should be:

> **To become the definitive digital infrastructure for discovering, evaluating, and connecting with legitimate businesses in Zambia.**

If we build it that way, we're no longer competing with a directory—we're creating a platform that businesses and customers rely on every day. That is a much bigger and more defensible vision.
